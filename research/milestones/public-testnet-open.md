# Canopy opens its public testnet after a 27,000-chain private test

**Verification status:** Verified
**Event date:** February 12, 2026
**Category:** Testnet launch

Canopy officially opened its **public testnet on February 12, 2026**, expanding access to the wider community. The official Canopy account says developers could then create and deploy custom **Layer-1 (L1) blockchains** through Canopy’s live portal. The same post reports that the preceding private testnet produced **nearly 27,000 chains in its first 12 days**. This supports the event as a transition from private testing to open builder participation, without adding claims beyond the sources.

The official post is dated **February 18, 2026**, but it explicitly states “On Feb. 12, Canopy officially opened its public testnet.” Therefore, the event date is supported at full day-level precision as **2026-02-12**; February 18 is the publication date, not the launch date.
