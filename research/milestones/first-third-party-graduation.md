# Canoliq becomes the first third-party app to graduate into its own network

- **Verification status:** Verified
- **Event date:** September 22, 2026
- **Category:** Ecosystem milestone
- **Candidate ID:** `first-third-party-graduation`

## Finding

The event is supported by Canopy's official Q3 2026 review, published October 1, 2026. It states that on **September 22**, Canoliq, a liquid-staking protocol, became the **first third-party app to graduate on the Canopy launchpad**. The same passage says it crossed from launch to an **independent network producing its own blocks**, with its **own explorer and wallet support**, in **about twelve minutes**.

This proves the candidate's date at full-day precision and supports the proposed factual description. The official post uses “first third-party app to graduate on the Canopy launchpad”; “into its own network” is a faithful condensation of the immediately following description of the independent network.

## Primary source

[Canopy Q3 2026 in Review](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/canopy-q3-2026-in-review) — Canopy Network, October 1, 2026.

Relevant passage (paraphrased): On September 22, Canoliq became the first third-party app to graduate on the Canopy launchpad; it moved from launch to an independent network producing its own blocks, with its own explorer and wallet support, in about twelve minutes.

## Supporting source

[The Last Step of a Launch Is No Longer Theory](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/last-step-of-a-launch) — Canopy Network, September 24, 2026.

This post identifies Canoliq as the first third-party chain to graduate on the Canopy launchpad and describes it as a liquid-staking protocol built by Nodefleet. It explains that graduation results in a live network producing its own blocks, the project's own block explorer and wallet support, and a transition that took about twelve minutes once it began. It says the graduation finished “on Tuesday,” which is consistent with the September 22 date explicitly stated in the Q3 review.

## Additional checked source

[Canoliq Is Live: Liquid Staking Arrives on Canopy](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/canoliq-is-live-liquid-staking-arrives-on-canopy) — Canopy Network, September 24, 2026.

This independently repeats that Canoliq was the first app built by a team outside Canopy to graduate from the Canopy launchpad, going from a project inside Canopy Terminal to an independent network in about twelve minutes. It further specifies that Canoliq began producing its own blocks, gained its own explorer and wallet support, and passed into its team's hands; once the crossing started, it took about twelve minutes.

## Verification conclusion

The proposed event, title, date, and description are verified from official Canopy sources. No broadening of the date is necessary.
