# Canopy mainnet goes live

**Verification status:** Verified
**Event date:** September 7, 2026
**Category:** Mainnet launch
**Primary source:** [Canopy Mainnet Is Live](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/canopy-mainnet-is-live)
**Supporting source:** [Canopy Q3 2026 in Review](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/canopy-q3-2026-in-review)

## Finding

The event occurred on **September 7, 2026**. Canopy’s official launch post is titled **“Canopy Mainnet Is Live”** and is dated September 7, 2026. It states, “Canopy mainnet is live,” and explains that work previously done on testnet now happens for real: applications are sovereign, their economics are real, and what builders launch is theirs.[1]

The post further says that mainnet applications are live products with real users and real economics, secured by the network from the moment they exist. Its launch infrastructure description is supported by specific shipped capabilities: deployment in minutes with monitoring already wired in; a wallet and block explorer on every node; security from block zero; and a faster, leaner indexer with official hosted snapshots.[1]

Canopy’s Q3 review independently corroborates the exact date, stating: “On September 7, Canopy mainnet went live.” It repeats the transition from testnet practice to mainnet applications with real users, real economics, and network security from the first block. It also corroborates the built-in wallet and block explorer, faster indexer, hosted snapshots, and reduced node-sync burden.[2]

The candidate’s date is therefore proven at full precision. The factual description is supported, with the infrastructure wording treated as a concise paraphrase of the launch post rather than an unsupported expansion.
