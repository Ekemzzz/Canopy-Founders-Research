# Canopy Templates launches for AI-assisted L1 development

**Verification status:** Verified
**Date:** January 30, 2026
**Category:** Product release
**Official title:** *Canopy Templates: Built for the AI Development Era*

Canopy’s official blog post, dated **January 30, 2026**, announces “Canopy Templates,” describing it as a blockchain-development approach designed specifically for AI-assisted coding. The post says developers can work with AI to “vibe code” complete Layer-1 blockchains from scratch in minutes rather than months. [1]

The primary source states that Canopy compressed its complete chain implementation into **roughly 200 lines of readable, language-native code**. It characterizes the Templates as the actual logic of an L1 blockchain rather than a wrapper, and says the compact implementation is intended to be legible to both developers and AI systems. [1]

The post further says the virtual-machine layer is eliminated and Templates are available in **Go, TypeScript, Python, C#, and Kotlin**. It presents this as allowing developers to use familiar, mainstream languages and standard development workflows. It also describes a workflow in which a developer selects a Template, describes requirements to an AI assistant, collaborates on the implementation, and deploys a functioning custom L1 in a single session. These are the release’s stated capabilities and positioning, not an independent performance assessment. [1]

Finbold independently reported on **February 5, 2026** that Canopy was launching Canopy Templates. Its report corroborates the approximately 200-line L1 implementation, AI-assisted design and deployment of custom blockchains, removal of the virtual machine, mainstream-language support, and the claim that operational blockchains can be delivered within minutes. [2]

The exact event date is supported by the date printed on Canopy’s primary announcement, so no date broadening is needed.
