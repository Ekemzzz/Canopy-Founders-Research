## Concise biography

Eric Nielson is a software and infrastructure engineering leader listed by Canopy as a Founding Engineer. Canopy’s April 1, 2026 article identifies him as a lead engineer and documents his end-to-end demonstration of building and launching an onchain prediction-market app with Canopy Templates and Claude Code. His public LinkedIn profile describes him as an experienced technical leader focused on secure, reliable enterprise systems; it lists current senior engineering management at Adobe and earlier engineering, architecture, consulting, and software roles.

## Documented Canopy contributions

Canopy’s April 1, 2026 article says Eric built a fully functional onchain Polymarket-style prediction-market app with Claude in about 20 minutes, end to end. The article describes him turning a rough idea into a specification, mapping it to a Canopy Template architecture, defining state models and transaction types, generating the onchain module and frontend, and then using the Canopy App to launch the chain. It says he used the Go Template, connected a GitHub repository, configured chain and branding details, and launched the chain with a total runtime of about 20 minutes. ([Official Canopy article](https://www.canopynetwork.org/blogs/vibecode-launch-a-blockchain-in-just-20-minutes-with-canopy))
