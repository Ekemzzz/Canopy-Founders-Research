## Evidence-backed profile

Andrew Nguyen is a technical founder and blockchain infrastructure engineer. His public professional profile describes a focus on L1 protocol design, Ethereum ecosystem development, and Web3 systems architecture. Before Canopy, the same profile lists him as a Founding Engineer at Pocket Network (May 2018–December 2022), Founder + Ethereum/React Developer at artpiece.io (February 2018–February 2019), and Blockchain Engineer at Nonce+1 Labs (December 2017–March 2018). It lists a BS in Computer Science from the University of South Florida (2014–2018). These dates and descriptions are reported as the profile presents them; no additional independent biographical details were added.

## Documented Canopy work and public appearances

- Andrew is named with Adam Liposky and Shawn Regan as a co-author of **_Canopy: An AI-Native Platform for Sovereign Blockchain Networks_**, Version 2.0 (June 2026). The whitepaper presents Canopy’s developer stack, Nested Chains, shared security, consensus, interoperability, and chain-halt-rescue design, but does not assign individual sections to each author.
- Canopy’s January 6, 2026 year-end post says Andrew and Adam held their first Canopy AMA in Discord during the latter half of Q4 2025. It also says Andrew appeared on the Brave New Coin Podcast to explain Canopy’s leading technical aspects.
- Brave New Coin’s December 16, 2025 podcast page identifies Andrew as Canopy’s Co-Founder and CTO and provides the episode page and downloadable MP3. The episode explains Canopy’s app-specific-chain / Nested Chain model and shared security concept.
- An IBTimes contributor-content report published February 18, 2026 identifies Andrew as Co-Founder and CTO and quotes him discussing the private testnet’s validation of easier L1 deployment, reduced friction, and AI collaboration.
