## Concise biography

Adam Liposky is a web3 infrastructure founder and the CEO and co-founder of Canopy Network. His public professional profile records Canopy leadership from November 2023. In a December 2025 Cryptopolitan interview, Liposky described moving from venture capital into crypto, building on Ethereum, and then working with Pocket Network before co-founding Canopy with Andrew Nguyen. These career details are attributed to the interview and profile rather than presented as independently audited employment history. [4] [5]

## Background facts

- His public LinkedIn profile lists a **Master of Science in Entrepreneurship in Applied Technologies** from the University of South Florida Muma College of Business, and bachelor’s studies at Florida State University in finance and entrepreneurship. [4]
- LinkedIn lists him as an **Investor at Feld Ventures** beginning December 2024, and previously as a **Techstars Web3 mentor** and **Founder/Managing Director of Skylabs**. These are profile-recorded roles; the note does not infer that any role is current unless the profile marks it current. [4]
- In the independent Cryptopolitan interview, Liposky said he spent years as a venture capitalist, became interested in crypto after receiving Ethereum-related material, left the fund to build on Ethereum, and later joined the Pocket Network team. [5]

## Documented Canopy contributions and public appearances

- Liposky is one of the three named authors of Canopy’s official **“An AI-Native Platform for Sovereign Blockchain Networks”** whitepaper, version 2.0, dated **June 2026**, alongside Andrew Nguyen and Shawn Regan. The paper presents Canopy’s architecture, including the Security Root, nested chains, shared/restaked security, the Canopy Stack, and Canopy Terminal. Authorship is documented; the paper alone does not establish which author implemented each individual component. [2]
- Canopy’s January 6, 2026 official year-end update says that co-founders **Adam Liposky and Andrew Nguyen held the team’s first Canopy AMA** in Discord in late 2025. [3]
- The same official update says Liposky appeared on the **BlockHash Podcast**, discussing his path to Canopy and the problems Canopy aims to solve. [3]
- Liposky was interviewed by Cryptopolitan on December 23, 2025. The article identifies him as Canopy’s co-founder and CEO and records his explanations of Canopy’s nested-chain model, shared validator security, sovereignty, native-token value capture, NestBFT, proof-of-age, and one-click L1 deployment. These are interview statements and product explanations, not independent technical validation. [5]
